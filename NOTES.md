# Teaching Notes & Learner Profile

## Learner Profile
- **Current Level:** Pre-Intermediate (A2 / B1- threshold).
- **Core Priority:** Conversational fluency, removing hesitation/language barrier, spontaneous speech.
- **Preferred Format:** 10–15 min interactive, self-contained HTML lessons with built-in exercises and active recall.

## Methodological Strategy
1. **Lexical Chunks & Sentence Stems:** Focus on complete conversational phrases (*"To be honest...", "I'm thinking of -ing...", "Have you ever considered...?"*) rather than isolated words.
2. **Retrieval Practice:** Every lesson must include active generation (filling blanks, oral transformation prompts, speed response quizzes).
3. **Conversational Scaffolding:** Teach active listening responses (*Rejoinders: "Really?", "No way!", "That makes sense"*) to keep conversations flowing effortlessly.
