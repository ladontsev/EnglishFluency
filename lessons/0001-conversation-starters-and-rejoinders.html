Lesson 0001 • Conversational Fluency

Destroying the Pause: Rejoinders & Buying Time

⏱ Estimated time: 10 mins 🎯 Level: Pre-Intermediate → Intermediate ⚡ Goal: Stop the awkward silence

1. The Secret to Native-like Fluency: Rejoinders

When someone speaks to you in English, you don't need a 3-paragraph answer right away. Native speakers keep conversations smooth and natural using Rejoinders — short 1–3 word reactions that signal: "I'm listening, and I understand you."

💡 The Golden Rule of Ping-Pong:
Instead of translating your answer in silence for 5 seconds, drop an instant Rejoinder, use a Time Buyer, and then speak your mind!
"That makes sense."
«Это логично / понятно»
"We decided to postpone the launch." → "Ah, that makes sense."
"Fair enough."
«Справедливо / согласен с доводами»
"I couldn't come because I was sick." → "Fair enough, get well!"
"No way!"
«Да не может быть! / Да ладно!»
"They doubled our budget!" → "No way! That's awesome."
"To be honest..."
«Честно говоря...» (Идеально, чтобы выиграть 2 секунды)
"What do you think of this idea?" → "To be honest, I need to test it."
