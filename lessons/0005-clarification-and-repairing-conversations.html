Lesson 05 • Repairing Communication

Saving the Call: Clarification & Paraphrasing

⏱ Estimated time: 12 mins 🎯 Level: Pre-Intermediate → Intermediate ⚡ Goal: Never panic or say "What?" when you miss something

1. The "What?!" Trap When You Didn't Catch It

In fast conversations or Zoom calls, missing a phrase happens all the time — even to native speakers! The difference is in how you ask for clarification:

❌ Sounds Rude / Unprofessional:
"What?" / "Repeat please." / (Awkward panic & silence)
✅ Confident & Smooth:
"Sorry, could you run that by me one more time? You broke up for a second."
🎯 Pro-Mindset: Asking for clarification with confidence makes you look more fluent and professional, not less!

2. 4 Essential Lifesaver Chunks

Use these patterns to smoothly steer the conversation back on track:

"Could you run that by me again?"
«Не мог бы повторить еще разок?» (Гораздо живее, чем "repeat")
"Sorry, you broke up. Could you run that by me one more time?"
"So what you're saying is..."
«То есть, ты имеешь в виду, что...» (Парафраз)
"So what you're saying is we should focus on the backend first?"
"Just to make sure I've got this..."
«Просто чтобы убедиться, что я правильно всё понял...»
"Just to make sure I've got this right, our next sync is on Friday?"
"What do you mean by...?"
«Что именно ты имеешь в виду под...?»
"What exactly do you mean by 'legacy code' here?"

4. Clarification Simulator

Choose the most professional and idiomatic response:

"(Fast and muffled speech): ...so we gotta wrap up the PR by EOD tomorrow, got it?"
"The product manager gave a long 5-minute explanation about feature requirements."

💡 The Zoom Call Emergency Formula

When audio lags or you lose track during a remote call, use this natural buffer:

"Sorry, you cut out for a second. Just to make sure I've got this right, are you saying we should wait for the new token?"
