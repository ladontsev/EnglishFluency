1. The 5 Idioms You Hear in Every Tech & Business Chat

Instead of memorizing hundreds of obscure idioms like "raining cats and dogs", master the handful of expressions that native speakers use daily in meetings, Slack, and calls:

🎯 Natural Idioms vs. Textbook English:
Instead of saying "Let us finish our work for today", a native speaker will almost always say: "Let's call it a day!"

2. 4 Core Conversational Idioms

Practice these idioms with their natural rhythms:

"Call it a day"
«Закругляться / заканчивать работу на сегодня»
"We solved the main bug, let's call it a day."
"Touch base"
«Связаться / быстро созвониться / сверить часы»
"I'll check the logs and touch base with you after lunch."
"Play it by ear"
«Действовать по ситуации / импровизировать»
"We don't have a fixed plan yet, let's just play it by ear."
"On the same page"
«Быть на одной волне / одинаково понимать задачу»
"Let's review the requirements so everyone is on the same page."

4. Idiomatic Situation Simulator

Choose the most natural, authentic reaction:

"It's 7 PM on a Friday and we've been debugging for 6 hours straight."
"Do you want to plan every hour of our Saturday trip in advance?"

🎧 Pro-Combo: The End-of-Meeting Wrap-up

Listen to how these idioms combine into a 10-second wrap-up:

"Great sync! Just to make sure we're on the same page, I'll send the summary and we'll touch base on Wednesday. Let's call it a day!"
