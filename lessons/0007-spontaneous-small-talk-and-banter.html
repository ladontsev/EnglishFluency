Lesson 07 • Casual Banter

Effortless Small Talk: The 2-Minute Pre-Meeting Chat

⏱ Estimated time: 12 mins 🎯 Level: Pre-Intermediate → Intermediate ⚡ Goal: Never feel awkward during the first 2 minutes of a call

1. The "How are you?" Trap

Non-natives often fear small talk because they think they need to give a medical or life report. In reality, English small talk is just a warm, low-pressure ritual to establish rapport:

❌ Overthinking / Stiff:
"I am fine, thank you, and how are you doing today?" (Textbook robot)
✅ Relaxed & Natural:
"Can't complain! Keeping busy. How's your week shaping up?"

2. 4 Natural Icebreakers & Replies

Use these friendly phrases to start or reply to casual banter:

"How's your week shaping up / going?"
«Как проходит / складывается неделя?»
"Hey Alex! How's your week shaping up?" → "Pretty good, lots of meetings!"
"Can't complain, keeping busy!"
«Не жалуюсь, весь в делах!» (Идеальный универсальный ответ)
"How are things?" → "Can't complain, keeping busy! And yourself?"
"Up to anything exciting this weekend?"
«Есть интересные планы на выходные?» (Пятничный хит)
"Happy Friday! Up to anything exciting this weekend?"
"Just taking it easy / catching up on rest"
«Ничего особенного, просто отдыхаю / чиллю»
"What did you do on Saturday?" → "Nothing crazy, just took it easy."

4. Small Talk Simulator

Choose the response that creates a warm, relaxed connection:

"Hey! Good to see you. How's everything going with you?"
"It's Friday afternoon on a Zoom call waiting for others to join."

🎧 The 10-Second Pre-Meeting Banter

Listen to a natural pre-meeting interaction:

— "Hey! How's your week treating you?"
— "Can't complain, keeping busy! Just looking forward to the weekend. How about you?"
